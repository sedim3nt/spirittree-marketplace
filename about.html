Why SpiritTree exists

SpiritTree started as a personal operations experiment: what happens when you replace most of your SaaS stack with custom AI agents? The answer — after months of real deployments — is that it works better than expected and costs a fraction of what subscriptions extract.

The products in this marketplace are the distilled artifacts of that experiment. Not theoretical guides. Not AI-generated filler. Each one is derived from actual workflows running on the SpiritTree infrastructure stack — the same multi-agent systems I use daily.

The consulting tiers exist because the map isn't the territory. If you want help navigating your own transition — from scattered SaaS tools to owned, compounding AI systems — that's the work. Book a roadmap session and let's build something real.
