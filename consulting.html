Questions

What makes this different from setting up AI myself?

Same difference as buying a server vs. hiring a sysadmin. We've spent months running an AI-operated business in production. You get battle-tested configurations, a working memory system, and the expertise to avoid the mistakes that take most people weeks to debug.

What do I need to provide?

A Claude Code subscription ($100-200/mo, depending on your usage) and answers to our intake questionnaire. We handle everything else — configuration, deployment, testing, and handoff.

How fast can my system be live?

Strategy Session delivers a written roadmap within 48 hours. Build tiers go live within 7 business days of the strategy call. Some simpler automations ship in 48 hours.

What does the $500/mo cover on Full Operations?

Hosting on dedicated infrastructure, monitoring, bug fixes, active improvement, and a monthly strategy call. We don't just keep the lights on — we make your system better every month based on your feedback and everything we learn across our operations.

Can I cancel Full Operations?

Month-to-month. No contract. If you leave, we hand over every file, every config, every piece of the system. Zero lock-in. We believe people stay because the service is good, not because they're trapped.

Is my data safe?

Full Ops clients run on isolated infrastructure. Your API keys are encrypted environment variables. Your business data flows through your system's runtime but is never logged, stored, or accessed by us. We maintain the AI layer — you own the data.

Can the $500 Strategy Session fee be applied to a higher tier?

Yes. If you upgrade to Roadmap + Build or Full Operations, your $500 is credited toward the setup cost.

Zero Lock-In Guarantee

You own everything we build. Every configuration file, every workflow, every piece of your system comes with you if you leave. We don't hold your data hostage. We earn your business every month.
