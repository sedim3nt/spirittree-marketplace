The Solopreneur CEO

"Your AI runs your entire solo operation"

Executive Productivity Persona Version 1

About

Most non-technical founders waste their sharpest morning hours on the wrong things — checking email, wondering what to prioritize, piecing together what happened yesterday. The Solopreneur CEO persona transforms your AI into a full chief of staff that handles the operational scaffolding so you can focus on actual decisions and creative work.

The morning brief is designed around decisions needed, not status updates. Every day at 7am you receive a structured document: what requires a choice from you today, what's overdue, what meetings are coming, and what's been accumulating in your open loops. It's the difference between an assistant that reports on things and one that surfaces what actually needs you.

Email triage runs every 8 hours using himalaya CLI. The system reads your inbox, categorizes each message against a learned profile of what you handle yourself versus what can be answered with a template, and what genuinely requires your attention. Routine queries get answered with your pre-approved templates. Complex or sensitive threads get forwarded to you with a 2-sentence context summary so you can respond quickly. You never wade through the full inbox for routine messages again.

The 7-day First Week Playbook is the piece most people skip in other products and the piece that makes this one actually work. It walks you through customization day by day: Day 1 is identity setup, Day 2 connects email, Day 3 handles calendar integration, Day 4 delivers your first real morning brief, Day 5 sets up the task prioritization system using the Eisenhower matrix adapted for AI workflows, Day 6 runs your first weekly review, and Day 7 hands full autonomy to the system. By the end of the week, the agent is running on your actual context, not a generic template.

This was built for solo operators who don't have a team to delegate to — consultants, founders, freelancers, creators. People who are simultaneously CEO, COO, and often the only employee. The persona configuration creates a system that scales your attention rather than demanding more of it. Production-tested with real client workflows, real email volume, and real calendar complexity.

What Makes This Different

  • Decisions, not status reports. Most AI assistants summarize what happened. This one surfaces what requires you — the difference between a briefing document and an actual chief of staff.
  • 7-day onboarding built in. Other products hand you config files and wish you luck. The First Week Playbook walks you through customization so the system is calibrated to your real work by day 7.
  • Email triage with real templates. Not "draft a reply" on demand — a standing system that runs every 8 hours and handles your routine inbox without you touching it.
  • Weekly review that actually surfaces stuck items. The cron doesn't just recap; it cross-references open loops against what was completed to find things that fell through the cracks.
  • Built for solo, not small teams. The persona assumes you have no one to delegate to. Every system is designed for one person with full operational scope.
