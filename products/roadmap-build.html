How it works

We start with the Strategy Session — the same 60-minute deep dive and written roadmap. Then we take the #1 automation opportunity from your roadmap and build it end-to-end. You provide access to your infrastructure (or we help you spin up a VPS), and we configure, test, and deploy a working system within 7 business days.

What we build

One primary automation system with up to 3 connected workflows. This could be a content pipeline (research, write, publish), an email triage system, a customer support flow, an ops automation — whatever ranked highest on your roadmap. One system, working end-to-end, with real data flowing through it.

What's included

  • Everything in the Strategy Session ($500 value)

  • Up to 3 connected workflows configured
  • Agent configurations and memory architecture
  • Integration testing with your actual tools and data

Your infrastructure

We deploy on your machine or a VPS you own. If you don't have a server, we help you spin up a $20-40/mo VPS on Hetzner or DigitalOcean during the build. You own it, you control it, it's yours. You'll also need a Claude Code subscription ($100-200/mo depending on usage).

Who this is for

Startup founders and small teams who want hands-on help getting their AI operations running. You've seen what's possible — you want someone who's done it before to build it right the first time instead of spending weeks figuring it out yourself.

After delivery

14 days of bug fixes are included. After that, you can add an optional $200/mo support retainer for ongoing monitoring, fixes, and a monthly check-in call. Or you can run it yourself — we hand over everything and you're fully self-sufficient.
