The Financial Analyst

"Institutional-grade research every morning"

Finance Research Persona Version 1

About

Most retail investors and serious hobbyists spend 1–2 hours every morning piecing together market context from a dozen sources: financial Twitter, earnings calendars, macro newsletters, YouTube analyst channels. The Financial Analyst persona does this synthesis automatically and delivers a structured briefing before the market opens.

The persona is built around a core belief: research quality determines investment quality. The difference between an institutional desk and an individual investor isn't access to data — it's the structured synthesis of that data into a usable decision framework. This persona replicates the workflow. Configure your sectors, add your tickers to the watchlist, and the system handles the rest.

The macro briefing framework was built from 25 Raoul Pal transcript analyses — one of the most coherent long-term macro thinkers covering the intersection of AI, crypto, and traditional markets. The synthesis prompt extracts the key framework variables he tracks (liquidity cycles, dollar weakness, tech adoption curves) and applies them to current conditions. It's not parroting his views — it's using his framework as a structured lens. This has been running as a daily briefing since March 2026 and has materially improved the signal-to-noise ratio of the morning research process.

YouTube analyst commentary monitoring is a feature most research tools skip entirely. The system monitors a configurable list of YouTube channels for new uploads, extracts key claims and thesis points via transcript analysis, and incorporates them into the weekly synthesis. When a researcher you trust publishes new analysis, it shows up in your briefing — not buried in a subscription feed you'll forget to check.

This is not a trading bot. It doesn't make buy/sell recommendations. It's a research analyst persona — the tool that saves you 2 hours of morning reading and ensures you're making decisions from a complete picture rather than whichever source you happened to open first. The portfolio watchlist alerts you when tickers you care about have significant news or price movements; what you do with that information is entirely yours.

What Makes This Different

  • Macro framework, not just headlines. The Raoul Pal synthesis layer gives you structural context, not just daily news. You understand why something is moving, not just that it moved.
  • YouTube monitoring is included. Most research tools ignore video. Some of the best analyst commentary lives on YouTube. This monitors it systematically.
  • Production-tested since March 2026. This isn't a theoretical setup — it's a daily briefing system that's been running and improving for months.
  • Three-market synthesis. AI, crypto, and traditional markets treated as interconnected, not siloed. Liquidity flows between them; the briefing reflects that.
  • Configurable, not fixed. Your sectors, your tickers, your analyst list. The framework is the product; the content is yours to configure.
