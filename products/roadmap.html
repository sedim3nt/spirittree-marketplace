Strategy Session

Consulting

60-minute strategy call where we audit your current operations, identify the highest-ROI automation opportunities, and build a custom AI operations roadmap tailored to your business. This isn't a generic playbook — it's a focused session with an operator who runs this stack in production every day.

Before the call

When you book, we send a short workflow questionnaire (5 questions). This means we show up to the call already understanding your business, your tools, and your bottlenecks — so we spend zero time on basic discovery and all 60 minutes on strategy.

What you get

  • 60-minute deep-dive strategy session on Zoom, recorded
  • Written roadmap delivered within 48 hours
  • Current state audit — where you are and what's costing you time
  • Top 5 automation opportunities ranked by ROI
  • Phased implementation plan (week 1, month 1, month 3)
  • Tool and model recommendations specific to your stack
  • Full recording and transcript of the session
  • 2 weeks of email follow-up for questions about the roadmap

Who this is for

Founders, solopreneurs, and operators who know AI can save them serious time but need a clear map to get there. You're drowning in content, email, admin, or ops work — and you want someone who's done it to tell you exactly what to automate first and how.

What happens next

The roadmap itself often becomes the sales tool for the next step. When you see "Phase 1: Set up content automation pipeline — estimated 8 hours of configuration," you'll know whether you want to build it yourself or have us do it. If you upgrade to Roadmap + Build or Full Operations, your $500 is credited toward the setup cost.
