Stripe Revenue Dashboard

Real-time Stripe revenue tracking

💳 Skill  ·  Finance  ·  Ops  ·  v1.0

About

Most founders check Stripe by logging into the dashboard and refreshing it manually. That's not a system — that's anxiety. The Stripe Revenue Dashboard turns your AI agent into a real-time financial nerve center, delivering the numbers you actually need, when you need them, without you having to look.

Setup takes 15 minutes using your existing Stripe API key. Once configured, you get an automated daily revenue report delivered to Telegram every morning. It covers the previous 24 hours: gross revenue, transaction count, average order value, and a comparison against your trailing 7-day average. Spikes get flagged. Drops get flagged. You know before your coffee gets cold.

MRR calculation runs automatically for subscription-based products. Failed payment alerts fire the moment a charge fails — giving you the window to recover revenue before the customer churns. Customer lifetime value tracking accumulates over time, letting you identify which product lines generate the most durable revenue. Refund monitoring surfaces unusual refund patterns before they compound into bigger problems.

The revenue-vs-goal tracker lets you set monthly targets and watch daily progress. Anomaly detection uses a configurable threshold to flag unusual spikes or drops in real time — useful for catching fraud, viral moments, or broken checkout flows immediately. Payout tracking tells you exactly when money is moving from Stripe to your bank.

This skill is built on the exact Stripe integration we use to run our own marketplace, which currently has 50+ products and processes dozens of daily transactions. Everything in this kit has been in production since early 2026. It is not theoretical — it's the system we depend on.

Whether you're a solo operator, a small agency, or a SaaS founder, this turns your revenue data from a thing you check into a thing that tells you.
