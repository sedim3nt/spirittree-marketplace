Smart Email Outreach

Research-first, not spray-and-pray

📬 Skill  ·  Sales  ·  Marketing  ·  v1.0

About

Cold outreach fails for one reason: it's obvious you didn't do your homework. Generic opening lines, wrong role assumptions, irrelevant pitches. Recipients can tell in three seconds whether you researched them or just scraped their email from a list. Smart Email Outreach is built on the premise that research comes first, always.

Before writing a single word of outreach, your agent researches the prospect. Company background, funding stage, recent press, the prospect's role history, their public writing, mutual connections or context you share. This research phase takes 2-3 minutes per prospect and produces a brief that informs everything that follows. The agent then writes three angle variations for each prospect — different hooks, different emphasis, different tone — so you can choose or A/B test.

Follow-up sequences are one of the highest-leverage activities in outreach, and one of the most neglected. This skill includes a 3-touch follow-up framework with configurable timing and escalating value delivery. Touch 1 is the initial email. Touch 2 adds context or a resource. Touch 3 is a soft close or explicit ask. Reply detection monitors for responses and pauses sequences when someone replies — so you never follow up on an email that already got answered.

The pipeline tracker gives you a visual queue of every active outreach sequence with status, stage, and next action. Reply categorization classifies incoming responses: interested, not now, wrong person, unsubscribe, objection. Each category has a response playbook. The objection handling library covers 8 common objections with response frameworks you can adapt.

A/B testing infrastructure tracks which subject lines, opening angles, and CTAs get the best reply rates across your campaigns. Over time, the system learns what works for your specific audience and context. Domain reputation monitoring ensures your send patterns stay within safe limits and your domain doesn't end up on spam filters.

This is not a mass email tool. It's a precision tool for founders, consultants, and business developers who send fewer emails but want each one to land. Quality over volume, every time.

What Makes This Different

  • Research before writing, every time. The framework makes shortcuts impossible — you can't skip research and still use the system.
  • 3 angle variations per prospect. Different hooks for different contexts. Test what lands best without writing from scratch.
  • Reply-aware sequences. Detection pauses follow-up when someone responds. No more embarrassing double-sends.
  • 8 objection templates. The most common objections, with frameworks for turning them into conversations instead of dead ends.
  • Surgical, not mass. Designed for 5-20 high-value prospects, not 5,000 scraped emails. Quality-first by design.
