The Brand Voice Agent

"Writes in YOUR voice, not AI voice"

Content Marketing Persona Version 1

About

The single biggest problem with AI-assisted content is that it sounds like AI-assisted content. The same phrases appear in every AI-generated article — "delve into," "comprehensive," "leverage," "in today's rapidly evolving landscape." Readers recognize these patterns. They signal that no human was actually thinking when this was written. The Brand Voice Agent is designed to solve this at the system level, not the prompt level.

The setup process starts with your existing writing. Feed it 10 samples — articles, emails, tweets, whatever you have — and the Voice Profile Builder extracts your patterns: how long your sentences tend to run, how often you use questions, your rhythm, your preferred connective phrases, the personality traits that show up consistently across different contexts. The result is a voice DNA profile that lives in the persona config and gets applied to every piece of content the agent produces.

Platform-specific voice adaptation is a critical feature that most generic "write in my style" prompts miss. How you write on X is fundamentally different from how you write on Substack — not just length, but register, cadence, the assumptions you make about what the reader already knows. The platform adaptation guide covers X, Substack, LinkedIn, and email, with distinct configurations for each. The agent doesn't just shorten your Substack article for Twitter — it rewrites it for the medium.

The De-AI-ify pass is a specific post-processing step that checks every output for the pattern library of robotic AI writing: overused transition phrases, passive constructions that no human naturally uses, vague generalities that pad word count without adding information, the telltale list of three that ChatGPT defaults to. The pattern removal list is maintained and expandable as new AI-isms emerge.

This was built and battle-tested during a period of publishing 13 Substack articles in 2 weeks across a multi-platform distribution pipeline. The CONTENT-STANDARD.md quality checklist is the actual 10-point checklist used to gate every piece of content before it went live. Voice consistency was a hard requirement — not a nice-to-have — and this system delivered it at publishing velocity.

What Makes This Different

  • Voice DNA, not style instructions. Generic "write like me" prompts degrade over sessions. A voice DNA profile extracted from your actual writing is durable and consistent.
  • De-AI-ify is a systematic pass. It's not "avoid sounding like AI" in the prompt — it's a specific list of patterns checked against every output before delivery.
  • Platform-specific adaptation is real. X and Substack are different media with different audiences and different conventions. One adaptation config doesn't cover both.
  • CONTENT-STANDARD.md is battle-tested. Not a theoretical checklist — the exact criteria used to gate 13 published articles in a real production pipeline.
  • 5 before/after examples included. You can see exactly what transformation the De-AI-ify pass performs before you use it on your own content.
